Ludu Legends Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Ludu Legends Ludo game (Android package com.smronju.ludulegends) handles your information. Ludu Legends is developed and published by Mohammad Shoriful Islam Ronju, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

In short: Ludu Legends works fully offline. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Your saved game and settings stay on your phone.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in the app’s private storage:

  • Game progress: your saved game, so you can continue it later.
  • Settings: language, sound and vibration preferences, and the setup you used last.

This information is used only to run the game. I never receive it.

2. What Ludu Legends does not do

  • No internet permission and no network requests.
  • No accounts and no developer-run servers.
  • No ads, analytics or crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

3. Device permissions

PermissionWhy Ludu Legends uses it
VibrateFor haptic feedback when you roll the dice and move pawns. You can turn it off in Settings.

4. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Ludu Legends’ saved game and settings in your backup to your Google account, or in a device-to-device transfer, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, not by me, and you can turn it off in your device’s system settings.

5. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your saved game and settings. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

6. Children’s privacy

Ludu Legends is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email smronju@gmail.com.
