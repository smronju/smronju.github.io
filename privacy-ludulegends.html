Ludu Legends Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Ludu Legends Ludo game (Android package com.smronju.ludulegends) handles your information. Ludu Legends is developed and published by Mohammad Shoriful Islam Ronju, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

In short: Ludu Legends has no accounts, and I never receive your personal data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information from your device to serve them, as described below. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Ads

The game shows a banner ad and a full-screen ad at the end of each match and, only when you choose to watch one, a video ad for free or doubled points. They are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information, such as model, OS version and language;
  • how you interact with the ads, and diagnostics about how they perform.

This collection is done by Google under Google’s privacy policy, including how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Your advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, on most Android phones).

2. Purchases

You can buy Remove ads, the Metal theme, or the Legend Pack (no ads, the Wood theme and 2,000 points). Points can also unlock themes; they stay on your device and are never sold or sent anywhere. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy; I never see your payment details. The game asks Google Play which of its products your Google account owns, and keeps that list on your device so ads stay off while you’re offline.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in the app’s private storage:

  • Game progress: your unfinished game for each way to play, so you can continue later.
  • Statistics: your games played and won, captures, and points.
  • Settings: language, sound and vibration preferences, your theme, and the setup you used last.
  • Purchases: which of the game’s products you own.

This information is used only to run the game. I never receive it.

4. What Ludu Legends does not do

  • No accounts and no developer-run servers.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files.
  • No sale of personal information.

5. Updates

When you tap Check for updates, the game uses Google Play’s in-app update library to ask the Google Play app on your phone whether a newer version is out, and to install it. That check is between Google Play and Google, under Google’s privacy policy.

6. Device permissions

Advertising IDRead by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads.

7. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Ludu Legends’ saved games and settings in your backup to your Google account, or in a device-to-device transfer, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, not by me, and you can turn it off in your device’s system settings. Purchases are not in the backup: Google Play restores them to your account.

8. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your saved games, statistics and settings. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on my side. To delete data Google holds, see Google’s privacy policy.

9. Children’s privacy

Ludu Legends is a general-audience game and is not designed for children. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email smronju@gmail.com.
