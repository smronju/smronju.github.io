LightUp

Android · Puzzle game

Close the circuit. Light the bulb.

A neon circuit-board puzzle. Tap hexagonal tiles to rotate them and connect the battery to the bulb. When the path closes, the circuit lights up.

how to play

  1. Find the battery and the bulb

    Each board has a power source and a bulb waiting to be lit.

  2. Rotate the tiles

    Tap any hexagonal tile to rotate it and line up the wires.

  3. Close the circuit

    Connect an unbroken path from battery to bulb and watch the board light up.

  4. Go for three stars

    Solve in as few taps as possible to earn every star.

privacy

Your game progress and settings are stored on your phone. LightUp has no server and no account of its own. It uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards, and Google AdMob to show ads between levels (never before level 5 or during daily puzzles).
