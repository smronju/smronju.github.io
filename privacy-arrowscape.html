Arrowscape Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Arrowscape puzzle game (Android package com.smronju.arrowscape) handles your information. Arrowscape is developed and published by Mohammad Shoriful Islam Ronju, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

In short: your game progress and settings are stored on your phone. Arrowscape has no server and no account of its own. It uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements and a leaderboard, and Google AdMob to show ads; those Google services collect some data as described below. The game may also download new level packs. I never receive your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Arrowscape saves the following locally in the app’s private storage:

  • Game progress: the levels you have cleared, the level you played last, your best result per level (moves, hearts lost and stars), the Daily Challenges you have won, how many Chaos rounds you have won, and which tutorials you have seen.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, theme, colour-blind patterns, and whether the daily reminder is on, its time and your quiet hours.
  • Level packs: a newer level pack, if one has been downloaded (see section 4).

This information is used only to run the game. I do not collect, transmit, sell or share it.

2. Google Play Games Services

Arrowscape uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements and a daily streak leaderboard. When you open the game, it tries to sign you in to Play Games automatically using your Google Play Games profile. You can also sign in from Settings, and you can play without signing in.

When you are signed in, Google receives and stores your achievement unlocks and your longest Daily Challenge streak. Your Play Games display name, avatar and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. You control your Play Games profile visibility in the Google Play Games app.

This data is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. I can see aggregate achievement and leaderboard statistics in the Google Play Console, but I do not receive your email address or other Google account details.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrowscape is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads may appear between levels, and you may be offered an optional rewarded video ad (for example, for an extra heart) that only plays when you tap to watch it.

To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your settings and location, ads may be personalized. This data goes directly to Google and is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or another region where consent is required, Arrowscape shows Google’s consent form before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice later from Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Google / privacy settings.

4. Level pack updates

Arrowscape may check for and download a newer pack of levels from a web server I host. The request contains no information about you or your progress; like any web request, the server sees your IP address and basic request details, which are used only to deliver the file. The pack is checked on your phone before it is used.

5. Device permissions

PermissionWhy Arrowscape uses it
Internet and network stateUsed by Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob and level pack updates.
NotificationsFor the optional daily reminder that a new Daily Challenge is ready. It is off by default; when you turn it on in Settings, Android 13 and later ask for your permission. Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device and never arrive during the quiet hours you set.
Run at startup, wake lock and foreground serviceAdded by Android’s WorkManager library so the daily reminder stays scheduled after your phone restarts.

Arrowscape does not access your device’s location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts or files.

6. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may automatically include Arrowscape’s saved progress and settings in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, not by me, and you can turn it off in your device’s system settings.

7. What Arrowscape does not do

  • No accounts of its own and no developer-run servers that store your data.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No precise location.
  • No sale of personal information.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling Arrowscape, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes your saved progress and settings.
  • Play Games data: you can delete your achievements and leaderboard data for Arrowscape, or your whole Play Games profile, from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.
  • Ad data: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

I do not run a server that stores data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete on my side, but you are welcome to contact me with any request.

9. Children’s privacy

Arrowscape is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact me.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s release notes.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email smronju@gmail.com.
