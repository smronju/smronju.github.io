Arrow Slip Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Arrow Slip puzzle game (Android package com.smronju.arrowslip) handles your information. Arrow Slip is developed and published by Mohammad Shoriful Islam Ronju, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

In short: Arrow Slip has no ads, no analytics, no accounts of its own and no servers, and I never receive your personal data. Your progress stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is optional: if you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google.

1. Achievements and leaderboards (Google Play Games)

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional; the game works fully without it. If you have Play Games on your phone, Google may sign you in automatically with your Play Games profile, or you can sign in from the Me screen. When you are signed in, the game tells Google which achievements you have unlocked or progressed (such as reaching a level, clearing levels without losing a heart, collecting stars, or finishing daily challenges) and sends three leaderboard scores: the highest level you have reached, your total stars, and your best daily-challenge streak. Google shows these on the leaderboards and on your Play Games profile, with your Play Games name and avatar, to other players as your Play Games settings allow. The Play Games software on your phone may also send Google diagnostics about how it is working.

This is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. I never see your Play Games profile or your Google account, and nothing is used for ads. You can change who sees your Play Games profile, or delete it and its game data, in the Play Games app or at Google My Account.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in the app’s private storage:

  • Game progress: your current level, the levels you have unlocked, the stars and best times you have earned, the daily challenges you have finished, and any board you are part-way through.
  • Settings: sound, vibration, theme, ruler and statistics preferences.
  • Reminders: whether the game has already asked for permission to show notifications.

This information is used only to run the game. I never receive it.

3. Daily reminders

The game can remind you, in the morning and in the evening, to play the daily challenge. The reminder is scheduled and shown entirely on your phone, only if you have not finished that day’s challenge, and only if you allow notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s notification settings for Arrow Slip.

4. What Arrow Slip does not do

  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No accounts of its own (Play Games sign-in is Google’s) and no developer-run servers.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

5. Device permissions

PermissionWhy Arrow Slip uses it
NotificationsTo show the daily-challenge reminders. Android asks you first, and you can say no.
Run at startupTo set the reminders again after your phone restarts or the game updates.
VibrateFor haptic feedback when you tap arrows. You can turn it off in Settings.

6. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Arrow Slip’s progress and settings in your backup to your Google account, or in a device-to-device transfer, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, not by me, and you can turn it off in your device’s system settings.

7. Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress in the game erases your progress. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it saved. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on my side. Achievements and scores already sent to Google Play Games can be deleted with your Play Games profile, as described in section 1.

8. Children’s privacy

Arrow Slip is a general-audience game and is not designed for children. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email smronju@gmail.com.
